LPM ships the account, settings, security, and development infrastructure most projects bolt together from separate tools as first-class commands. Everything in this section assumes [`lpm dev`](/docs/dev/dev) is your default; these commands are what `lpm dev` orchestrates under the hood and what you reach for when you need to inspect, configure, or fix something it touched.
